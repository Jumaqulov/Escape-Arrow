Privacy Policy - Arrow Escape

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Arrow Escape is a single-player puzzle game published by Starlab LLC. This policy explains exactly what the game stores and which third parties are involved. It is short because the game does very little.

1. Personal data we collect

None. Arrow Escape does not ask for, collect, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personally identifying information. We operate no servers of our own and no account system.

2. Data stored on your device

The game keeps a small amount of game state so you do not lose your place:

  • Which levels you have unlocked and how many stars you earned on each
  • Your sound on/off preference
  • Your chosen interface language

This is written to your browser's localStorage under the key arrow-escape-save. It never leaves your device except as described in section 3. Clearing your browser data deletes it permanently.

3. Third-party platform SDKs

Arrow Escape is distributed through gaming portals. When the game is loaded from one of them, that portal's own SDK is loaded and is governed by that portal's privacy policy, not by ours:

  • Yandex Games SDK - used for cloud save of your progress, for advertising, and for the platform's own analytics. See Yandex's privacy policy.
  • CrazyGames SDK - used for advertising and for the platform's own analytics. Progress on CrazyGames is stored only in your browser's local storage. See CrazyGames' privacy policy.

Only one of these loads at a time, chosen automatically from the site the game is running on. When you run the game outside those portals, neither loads and no third-party code executes.

4. Advertising

Ads are served by the hosting portal, not by us. Interstitial ads appear between levels, and optional rewarded video ads can be watched in exchange for a hint or an undo. We receive no personal data from ad delivery. Ad personalisation, where offered, is controlled by the portal and by your account settings on it.

5. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and knowingly collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. Advertising is handled by the hosting portal under its own child-directed-content rules.

6. Your choices

  • Clear your browser storage for the site to erase all local progress.
  • Delete your saved data held by Yandex through your Yandex account settings.
  • Simply stop playing - nothing is retained by us afterwards, because nothing is sent to us.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@starlab.uz (Starlab LLC).
